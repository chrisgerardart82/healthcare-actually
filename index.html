CG
Chris GalatiotoHealthcare · Operations · Perspective
Get to know Chris Galatioto +

Chris Galatioto brings an operations-focused perspective to Healthcare Actually, connecting the everyday realities of healthcare with the bigger questions about how the system works. With a curiosity for the people, decisions, and processes behind care, Chris helps bring complex conversations down to earth. Alongside his co-host, he explores what is working, what needs rethinking, and why the people delivering and receiving care deserve to be part of the conversation.

Tim ClarkHealthcare · Operations · People
Get to know Tim Clark +

Tim Clark brings a grounded operations perspective to Healthcare Actually, with an interest in the people, decisions, and everyday realities that shape care. He explores the gap between how healthcare is designed to work and how it feels to deliver or experience it, asking practical questions and looking for ideas that can make the system better.
